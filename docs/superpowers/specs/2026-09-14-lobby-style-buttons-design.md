# Lobby-style buttons (2026-09-14)

Agreed through the /gamecode Brief. All three decisions took the bold
defaults.

## What the player gets

Every button in the game looks like the Lobby's STUDENT, JADWAL and nav
tiles, except the buttons on StudentCard. Each button keeps its size, text
and icon.

The Lobby look is the brand's warm-brown fill (`brand_primary_light` #9C6440
with the darker `brand_primary_dark` bevel), the cream `outline_card` rim, the
standard button radius and shadow, and cream `text_on_brand` text in the
display face.

## Where the look comes from today

`LobbyCtaButton` and `LobbyNavTile` are built by `_add_button_variation()`
with `brand_primary_light`, `brand_primary_dark`, `outline_card` and
`text_on_brand`. These are the same four arguments `PrimaryButton` uses. The
button styles that look different today are:

| Variation | Today | After |
|---|---|---|
| `SecondaryButton` (+M, +L) | cream fill, brown rim and text | Lobby |
| `DangerButton` (+M, +L) | red | Lobby (Decision 1) |
| `SuccessButton` (+L) | green | Lobby (Decision 1) |
| `MainMenuButton` | painted gold `menu_button.png` | Lobby (Decision 2), icon margins kept |
| `ShopShelfButton` | flat brown box, gold hover text | Lobby (Decision 2) |
| `ResultButton` | cream `card_bg.png` art | Lobby (Decision 2), 24 px side margins and 52 px text kept so SELANJUTNYA fits |

The size steps (`…M`, `…L`) are generated by `_add_size_step()` from their
base, so they follow the base automatically.

## Exceptions

- **StudentCard.** Its six Aprove buttons and the Belajar button use
  `PrimaryButtonL`, which already has the Lobby look. The eight
  `SecondaryButtonL` nodes (six `Batal` and the two page arrows) move to a new
  `StudentCardSecondaryButtonL` (base `StudentCardSecondaryButton`). It keeps
  today's cream secondary look exactly: `surface_card` to `surface_sunken`,
  `brand_primary` rim and text, and `font_h1` / `btn_pad_v_l` at the L step.
- **StudentList's BELUM/SUDAH status badges** (`RosterCard.tscn`). They move
  from `DangerButton` / `SuccessButton` to new `RosterStatusBelum` /
  `RosterStatusSudah` variations with today's red and green. The colour is
  the information they carry.
- **Unchanged, as they are not framed action buttons:** `GhostButton` (the
  daily-reward CLAIM on baked art), `ShopHubTile` (icon tiles),
  `FilterChipButton` (toggles), `EventSelectCard` (student pick cards),
  trait chips (`QuirkBadge`, `PersonaBadge`, `SpecialtyBadge`) and
  `CardArrowButton` (report-card arrows, already brand brown).

## Decisions, as agreed

1. Red and green action buttons turn Lobby brown: Keluar in
   `QuitConfirmDialog`, Menjodohkan's check and its lock/cancel button.
   Menjodohkan's lock state still reads through its label ("Kunci
   Jawaban!" / "Batalkan").
2. The textured buttons turn Lobby brown: the main menu's icon buttons,
   Koperasi's shelf button, and Weekly Results' Logs / Selanjutnya.
3. Paired choices (Terima/Tolak, Skip Dialog/Jangan Skip Dialog, …) look
   identical. The `confirm_pair_semantics` rule of one filled button and one
   quiet one is retired. The pairs keep their `PrimaryButton` /
   `SecondaryButton` names, so a later pass can split them again.

## State and grades

No game state is read or written, and the change is the same in every grade.
