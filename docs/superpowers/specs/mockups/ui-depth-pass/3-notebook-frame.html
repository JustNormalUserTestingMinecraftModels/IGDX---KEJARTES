What changed from the first notebook:
  • Brown hardcover behind the page, with a lip. This replaces the flat outline. The page also has two paper edges under it, so it reads as a thick pad.
  • Chunky rings. Each has a highlight and a dark underside, over real punched holes, crossing from the cover onto the page.
  • Tabs built like the buttons. Gloss on top, outlined white lettering in each tab's own lip colour. The active tab is taller, in front, and notched into the page.
  • The title is a stitched sticker (cream with a dashed border, slightly tilted), like the score badge in your second reference, instead of plain text.
  • Controls sit in a sunken well, the same way your second reference insets its chart panel. There's also a red margin line, a real school-notebook detail.
  • Washi tape has torn ends and uses the accent palette.
